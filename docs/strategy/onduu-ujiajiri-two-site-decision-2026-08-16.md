# Onduu Changes and Ujiajiri Partner Website Content

Prepared: 16 August 2026

## 1. Final two-site decision

The websites should serve different jobs.

```text
ONDUU.KE
Education + Digital Readiness + Demand Generation
        |
        +--> HOSTAFRICA for domains, hosting, email and VPS products
        |
        +--> UJIAJIRI.KE for youth pathways and independent delivery partners

UJIAJIRI.KE
Skills + Self-employment + Independent Partner Discovery
        |
        +--> Youth build practical capability
        |
        +--> Businesses choose and contract delivery partners directly
        |
        +--> Eligible youth/partners use official HOSTAFRICA programmes
```

Onduu should not claim to sell or manage website projects. Ujiajiri should not take client project payments or act as prime contractor in the recommended first model. HOSTAFRICA should remain the seller, biller, provisioner and supporter of its products.

## 2. Verified current-state problem

On 16 August 2026, `https://onduu.ke/` and `https://ujiajiri.ke/` returned byte-for-byte identical homepage HTML. Both had:

- title: **Website Revenue and Digital Readiness in Kenya | Onduu**;
- description: **Onduu helps established Kenyan businesses find and fix website weaknesses affecting enquiries, trust, control and resilience**;
- Onduu navigation, Readiness Score and Onduu footer.

This must be corrected. `ujiajiri.ke` needs its own deployment, metadata, sitemap, canonical URLs, analytics property/data stream and Search Console property. Do not merely change the logo while retaining identical copy.

# Part A — Required changes to Onduu.ke

## 3. New role for Onduu

Onduu becomes the trusted explanation and routing layer:

- publish practical content about websites, domains, hosting, email, VPS, data location and supervised AI-agent use;
- help businesses understand their digital-readiness gaps;
- direct infrastructure demand to an approved HOSTAFRICA destination;
- direct website-design and digital-marketing implementation to independent partners listed on Ujiajiri;
- document evidence, responsibilities and limitations;
- avoid contracting, invoicing, project-managing or guaranteeing implementation.

### New positioning statement

> Onduu helps Kenyan businesses understand what is weakening their digital operation, decide what to fix first and continue through the right delivery path.

### New one-line description

> Practical digital-readiness guidance for better website and infrastructure decisions.

### Primary CTA

**Check Your Digital Readiness**

### Secondary CTA

**Find an Implementation Partner**

### Infrastructure CTA

**Explore the HOSTAFRICA Path** — publish only after the exact destination and wording are approved.

## 4. Claims and sections to change immediately

### Replace direct-delivery language

Current language such as **“Onduu finds and fixes the weaknesses”** implies Onduu performs the work. Replace it with:

> **Onduu helps you identify the weaknesses, understand the priority and choose the right delivery path.**

### Remove or gate these claims

- Onduu fixes client websites.
- Onduu operates Managed Website Operations.
- Onduu provides ongoing monitoring, alerts or reports to clients.
- Onduu runs client AI agents or managed infrastructure.
- Onduu sells a Website Revenue System implementation.
- Onduu is responsible for partner quality, delivery or support.

These may return only if separately approved, resourced and contractually supported.

### Keep and reposition

- Digital Readiness Score: keep as an educational/qualification tool with clear limitations.
- Website Revenue System: keep as a published framework or guide, not an Onduu delivery promise.
- Insights: keep as the main content engine.
- Kenyan infrastructure and agent content: keep with approved HOSTAFRICA routes and clear responsibility boundaries.
- Labs/tools: keep behind safe data, approval and methodology controls.
